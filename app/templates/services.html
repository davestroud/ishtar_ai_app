LLM Performance Engineering

Squeeze maximum throughput and minimum cost from your LLM infrastructure through model-level, inference-engine, and system-level optimizations grounded in quantitative cost formulas.

  • Quantization (FP32→FP16→INT8→GPTQ) and PEFT/LoRA/QLoRA adapter serving
  • PagedAttention (vLLM) for 2–3× throughput via virtual KV cache paging
  • Speculative decoding for latency-sensitive endpoints
  • Dynamic batching, autoscale replica sizing, cost-per-1K-token modeling
  • KV cache quantization and prefix caching strategies

CI/CD & Evaluation for LLM Systems

Prompts, adapters, and schemas are production artifacts — treat them with the same rigor as application code. Behavioral guarantees, not just unit tests.

  • LLM-as-Judge with calibration protocol (Spearman ρ > 0.7 against human anchors)
  • Evaluation taxonomy: functional, semantic/RAG, adversarial, and behavioral regression
  • Golden datasets, adversarial probes, and statistical control chart gates (x̄ ± 2σ)
  • Canary → blue-green deployments with rollback in < 5 minutes
  • Bias/verbosity/position bias mitigations in judge rubric design

LLM Monitoring & Observability

Standard infrastructure metrics are not enough for LLM systems. Semantic drift, token-level cost attribution, and multi-step trace correlation require a purpose-built observability layer.

  • Semantic drift detection and output quality scoring (not just CPU/latency)
  • Token-level cost attribution per request, user, and feature
  • TTFT vs. end-to-end SLO tracking with streaming UX tradeoff analysis
  • RAG recall@K and faithfulness score continuous monitoring
  • Correlation IDs across multi-agent hops for deterministic replay and debugging
  • Hallucination spike incident playbooks with index rollback runbooks

Responsible AI & Governance

Responsible AI is an engineering discipline, not a policy checkbox. Bias audits, compliance mapping, and fairness metrics belong in your CI/CD pipeline from day one.

  • 5-level Responsible AI Maturity Model (Ad Hoc → Documented → Systematic → Governed → Optimized)
  • OWASP LLM Top 10 mitigations deployed in CI gates
  • EU AI Act compliance mapping and NIST AI RMF implementation
  • Automated bias audits, structured fairness metrics, and cross-regulation compliance matrix
  • Audit trails, PII leakage controls, and safety violation taxonomy enforcement
